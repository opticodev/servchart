# Code of Conduct

ServChart is built by people with different backgrounds, experience levels and first languages. We want taking part to be a good experience for everyone, whether you're filing your first issue or maintaining the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding someone is wrong or careless.
- Critique code and ideas, never people. Explain the "why" in reviews.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change that doesn't fit the project's scope, and decline other people's ideas respectfully yourself.
- Respect people's time: search before posting, keep discussions on topic.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, including details of their servers or infrastructure, without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging others to do any of the above.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat rooms) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers: through GitHub's private reporting (repository "Security" tab → "Report a vulnerability", with "Code of Conduct" in the title), or by email to the address on the [@opticodev](https://github.com/opticodev) GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, depending on how serious and repeated it is.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
